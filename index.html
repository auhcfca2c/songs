With “Fit to 1 page” on, all text shrinks by the same amount until everything fits, and the columns are balanced, breaking between verses where possible. All text is Arial 12, black; section headings on their own line, like (Verse 1) or (Chorus), are made bold automatically. Use “Import file…” or drop a PDF, Word (.docx) or PowerPoint (.pptx) file on the text box to bring in its text; scanned PDFs have no text to read. Fonts are the standard PDF set, which covers English and Western European characters. Other characters (for example Arabic) are replaced with “?”.

      Lyric slide builder

      Paste your lyrics under each section heading, and keep each heading like (Verse 1) on its own line. Each section is split into slides of up to 4 lines, shared evenly (6 lines become 3 + 3), unless you set a number below: first line in caps, the rest in normal case. Slides follow the order of the lyrics box; to repeat a section (like the Chorus), write its heading again, and leave it empty to reuse the same lyrics. An empty (Repeat All) repeats every section above it except the (Title), and an empty (Repeat Chorus) repeats the Chorus.
